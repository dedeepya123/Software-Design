# How to Recognise a Design Pattern

## Core Idea
- Patterns are recognised by the design problem they solve, not by their syntax.

## Recognition Process
1. Identify the object's responsibility.
2. Identify collaborators.
3. Identify dependencies.
4. Identify what varies.
5. Identify what stays stable.
6. Observe SOLID principles.
7. Infer the design pattern.

## Key Questions
- What is this object responsible for?
- Who collaborates with it?
- What depends on it?
- What does it depend on?
- What is expected to change?
- Can implementations be substituted safely?

## Key Insight
- Patterns describe recurring solutions to recurring design problems.
- The same pattern can have many different implementations.

## Mental Model
- Recognise patterns the way a detective recognises a case:
  collect clues, understand the problem, then identify the solution.

## Ask why this pattern was chosen over another.

### example:

- Why Strategy instead of inheritance?
- Why Factory instead of calling constructors directly?
- Why Decorator instead of adding another subclass?
- Why Composition instead of inheritance?
