# Introduction to Behavioral Patterns

## Purpose
- Behavioral patterns focus on how objects communicate, collaborate, and divide responsibilities once they already exist.

## Core Question
- How should objects interact to accomplish a task while remaining loosely coupled and maintainable?

## Compared to Other Categories
- Creational: How are objects created?
- Structural: How are objects organized?
- Behavioral: How do objects collaborate?

## Common Design Problems
- Choosing between different algorithms.
- Notifying multiple interested objects.
- Passing requests without tight coupling.
- Changing behavior based on state.
- Traversing collections safely.
- Defining common algorithm skeletons.

## Key Insight
- Behavioral patterns are about the flow of responsibility and communication between objects, not their creation or structure.
