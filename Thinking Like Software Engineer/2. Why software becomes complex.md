# Why Software Becomes Complex

## Complexity
- Complexity is the mental effort required to understand, reason about, modify, or predict the behavior of software.
- It is about human understanding, not computer execution.

## Key Insight
- Complexity grows primarily because of increasing interactions and dependencies, not simply because of more lines of code.

## Essential Complexity
- Comes from the problem domain itself.
- Cannot be eliminated, only managed.

## Accidental Complexity
- Introduced by poor design or implementation.
- Can and should be reduced.

## Technical Debt
- The future cost of shortcuts taken today.
- Sometimes intentional, but dangerous if left unpaid.

## Goal of Software Design
- Reduce accidental complexity.
- Organize essential complexity.
- Make software easier for humans to understand and evolve.

**Complexity comes more from interactions than from the number of components.**
