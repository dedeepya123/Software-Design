# Responsibilities & Object Collaboration

## Responsibility
- A responsibility is a piece of work that an object owns.
- Good objects have focused responsibilities.

## Collaboration
- Complex problems are solved by multiple objects working together.
- Objects collaborate instead of trying to do everything themselves.

## Delegation
- Delegation means asking another object to perform work that belongs to it.
- It keeps responsibilities in the right place.

## Tell, Don't Ask
- Prefer telling an object what to do instead of reading its internal state and making decisions externally.
- Let objects protect and manage their own state.

## Information Expert
- Assign a responsibility to the object that already has the information needed to perform it.

## Coordinator vs Worker
- Coordinator objects orchestrate workflows.
- Worker objects perform specialised tasks.

## Design Goal
- Systems become easier to understand, test, and change when responsibilities are assigned thoughtfully.

Mental Model
- Think of objects as specialists in an orchestra.
- Each has its own role, and together they produce the complete system.
